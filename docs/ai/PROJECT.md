# ClinicOS Project
Goal: commercialize a multi-tenant medical/dental SaaS with CRM, omnichannel communication, automation, and AI customer service.
Problem: clinics use fragmented systems for appointments, patient records, billing, CRM, calls, and social communication.
Users: owners, managers, clinicians, reception, assistants, accounting, service, marketing, patients.
Current stage: Master Specification / Pre-Implementation.
