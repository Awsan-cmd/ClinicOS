# Product Blueprint
## Pillars
1. Clinic operations
2. Patient experience
3. Revenue/CRM
4. Omnichannel communication
5. AI automation
6. Analytics
7. Security/reliability

## Personas
Owner: revenue, staffing, branches, control.
Clinician: schedule, patient context, notes, treatment/follow-up.
Receptionist: calendar, registration, communication, reminders.
Manager: operations and reporting.
Accountant: invoices, payments, balances.
Service agent: unified inbox, AI assistance, escalation.
Patient: booking, reminders, records/documents, communication, payments.

## Differentiation
AI-native omnichannel service, Arabic/English RTL-first, medical+dental, multi-branch, workflow automation, human+AI collaboration, provider-agnostic integrations, audit/security.
