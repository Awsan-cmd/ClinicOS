# Implementation Plan
## Phase 0 Foundation
Repository, docs/memory, TypeScript/tooling, Docker, env config, CI, test foundation.
## Phase 1 Platform
Database/migrations, tenancy, organizations/branches, auth, sessions, RBAC, audit.
## Phase 2 Clinic
Staff/providers, services, patients, calendar, scheduling, appointment lifecycle.
## Phase 3 Clinical
Encounters, notes, diagnoses/allergies/medications, attachments, templates.
## Phase 4 Dental
Odontogram, tooth records, treatment plans, dental workflow.
## Phase 5 Billing
Invoices, payments, receipts, balances, reports.
## Phase 6 CRM
Leads, pipeline, tasks, campaigns, follow-ups.
## Phase 7 Messaging
Conversation model, inbox, templates, notifications, webhooks.
## Phase 8 Channels
WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram/Facebook where supported, Email, Voice.
## Phase 9 AI
Knowledge base, AI gateway, tools, receptionist, booking, lead/follow-up/no-show, handoff, evaluation/safety.
## Phase 10 Automation/Analytics
Workflow engine, triggers/actions, dashboards, KPIs.
## Phase 11 Patient portal
Portal, booking, documents, messages, payments.
## Phase 12 Beta
Pilot clinics, monitoring, support, subscriptions, feedback.
## Phase 13 Production
Security hardening, backups/restore, capacity, incident response, documentation, release process.

Every phase must be split into small testable tasks.
